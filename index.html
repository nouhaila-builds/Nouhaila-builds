    An independent study of whether pixel fidelity, perceptual similarity, and pedestrian-detection utility fall together when a visible-to-infrared translator is trained on fewer aligned pairs. The data is LLVIP. A U-Net trained with L1 is the baseline. The main model is conditional diffusion: RGB is concatenated with the noisy thermal image, the network predicts the noise, and sampling takes 50 DDIM steps. The fractions are nested, 1% ⊂ 5% ⊂ 20% ⊂ 100%, after one frozen validation cut. The test set does not change. A pedestrian detector is trained on real infrared, then frozen.

    • LLVIPaligned visible–infrared pairs
    • 1% → 100%nested training fractions
    • 50DDIM sampling steps

    On this run, PSNR stayed low and flat, near 11 dB. SSIM, LPIPS, and pedestrian AP did not move together. Generated infrared is 128×128. One seed, 42.

    Python · PyTorch · U-Net · conditional diffusion · DDIM · LLVIP

    A vision pipeline that detects facades by joining a LiDAR point cloud with a photograph. YOLOv8 detects facades in the image. Open3D extracts vertical planes from the cloud. When a camera calibration exists, fusion projects those planes onto the image and keeps the detections that agree. KITTI-360 supplies the training images. nuScenes mini supplies a real camera–LiDAR calibration. Pseudo-labels are projected from the LiDAR onto the pictures.

    • LiDAR + imageone facade, two sensors
    • YOLOv82D detection
    • KITTI-360 · nuScenestraining set and calibration

    The 2025 version detected planes and boxes. The 2026 version adds KITTI-360, nuScenes calibration, and LiDAR-projected pseudo-labels. Weights and the large datasets stay off GitHub.

    Python · PyTorch · YOLOv8 · OpenCV · Open3D · nuScenes · KITTI-360

    Forecasting dispensed volume from Open Medic with pandas, NumPy, and a Jupyter pipeline. Rows are projected onto a calendar and aggregated into a daily series. Features are calendar cycles, lags, and rolling statistics. statsmodels, scikit-learn, and XGBoost share one chronological split — 70 / 15 / 15 — so the future never leaks into training. TensorFlow stays optional, and only for the LSTM.

    • Open Medicdaily volume series
    • 70 / 15 / 15time-ordered split
    • HybridARIMA + XGBoost

    The bench includes moving average, linear trend, single and double exponential smoothing, ARIMA, linear regression, random forest, XGBoost, a stacked hybrid, and an optional LSTM. Scores are MAE, RMSE, MAPE, and R².

    Python · pandas · NumPy · Jupyter · statsmodels · scikit-learn · XGBoost

    A four-class image model in TensorFlow and Keras that places a brain MRI into NonDemented, Very Mild, Mild, or Moderate dementia. scikit-learn builds the stratified 70 / 15 / 15 split. The public set holds about 44,000 already augmented scans. Class weights come from the training fold only, with early stopping on validation. The reference comparison trains SimpleCNN from scratch against an ImageNet ResNet50. VGG16, AdvancedCNN, and MultiScaleCNN are the other architectures on the same bench.

    • ~44kMRI scans
    • 4clinical stages
    • 224²input resolution

    Demonstration only. The set has no patient id, so an image and its augmentation can land in both train and test. It is not a diagnostic tool.

    Python · TensorFlow / Keras · scikit-learn · SimpleCNN · AdvancedCNN · MultiScaleCNN · ResNet50 · VGG16

    Afro-TB holds 13,681 African isolates of Mycobacterium tuberculosis across 25 countries and 9 lineages. pandas prepares the table. scikit-learn fits logistic regression, KNN, and a random forest on country and lineage alone, with NumPy, SciPy, matplotlib, and seaborn around the figures. The 157 resistance mutations already define the published drug profile, so the models never see them. They beat a majority-class baseline. The signal sits mostly in country, then lineage 2.

    • 0.775random forest AUC
    • 0.655F1, resistant class
    • 0.575majority baseline accuracy

    Logistic regression AUC 0.764, KNN 0.770. Country and lineage describe where an isolate comes from. They do not explain the resistance mechanism. South Africa and lineage 4 dominate the collection.

    Python · pandas · NumPy · scikit-learn · SciPy · matplotlib · seaborn · logistic regression · KNN · random forest

    An atlas in React and TypeScript, built with Vite, for one question: how climate risk changes across regions, hazards, and four decades. MapLibre draws the globe, D3 draws the fingerprint and the trends, Framer Motion handles the motion, and Zustand keeps the map, the playhead, and the selected country on one state. FastAPI and NumPy serve the series. Spain, extreme heat, and 2005–2025 follow the reader from the globe into a fingerprint, a trend, a comparison, and a written investigation. Up to four territories sit on the same scale.

    • 1980–2025complete years
    • ERA5observed temperature
    • 6hazard scales

    Temperature is ERA5 via Our World in Data. Heat days, drought, precipitation, flood, wildfire, and storm are a documented simulation and labeled as such. Missing years stay missing.

    React · TypeScript · Vite · MapLibre · D3 · Framer Motion · Zustand · FastAPI · NumPy

    A single investigation in React, TypeScript, and Tailwind, instead of five dashboards: Overview, Detect, Investigate, Trace. MapLibre, D3, and Plotly share one Zustand selection with the KPIs, the alerts, and the order timeline. FastAPI, pandas, NumPy, NetworkX, and scikit-learn aggregate every number from the tables. The analyst sees the health of a European network, then follows an anomaly down to one shipment. No sentence is written by a language model.

    • 30korders
    • 120kshipment legs
    • 79.6%on-time, vs 98.9% before

    The network is synthetic, seed 42, with five injected disruptions the detectors have to find from timestamps, cost, and stock. Detection is statistical: z-scores, an interquartile fence, and a linear cost residual.
